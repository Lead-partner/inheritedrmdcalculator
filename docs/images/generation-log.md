# Image generation and QA log

Checked 14 Nov 2026. No AI image generation was used; there are no prompts, AI images, rejected generations or third-party stock assets.

- **Data diagrams:** 13 original SVGs generated by `scripts/figures.mjs`. Divisors come from `data/irs-tables.json`, independently compared with all 121 Table I rows in the saved IRS Publication 590-B. Hypothetical balances, account names and death years are labelled as examples. Rule evidence maps to `sources-ref/article-evidence.json`.
- **Widget preview:** headless Chrome capture of the actual local `/embed/` at 560 × 640, resized with Pillow to 480 × 549 and 960 × 1097; delivered in AVIF and WebP. No synthetic interface artwork.
- **Social cards:** 14 original 1200 × 630 PNGs composed in HTML with each page’s lead SVG, widget screenshot or page-specific text, rendered in headless Chrome. Recreate with `npm run build && npm run images`.
- **QA:** browser bounding-box checks detect clipped SVG labels; automated tests verify image paths, alternative text, dimensions, social metadata, schema images, sitemap images, source evidence and every plotted Table I point. Desktop/mobile screenshots and Lighthouse results are recorded in the QA report.
- **Licence:** original diagrams, screenshot and social compositions are project artwork under the repository MIT licence. IRS facts and US federal government source material are public domain. Source Serif 4 and Public Sans retain their bundled SIL Open Font Licence files in `static/fonts/`. No coins, photographs or generated accents were used.

QA verdict: PASS after correcting one overlong Roth diagram heading. All diagrams use the site palette and fonts, and the hero requires no raster image.
